5. Caveats & Limitations

Identification is pattern-only: no elemental (EDX/XRF) chemistry or metadata was provided to confirm Ca, so the assignment rests on band positions and relative intensities alone (though these are strongly self-consistent for aragonite). The dominant 1085 band retains a dispersive/cusp residual at its apex (~±1000 counts) that even a skewed pseudo-Voigt did not fully capture — reported FWHM (~3.4 cm-1) and asymmetry_ratio (0.52) for that band are therefore approximate and may reflect an instrumental/apex-sharpening artifact rather than pure physical lineshape. Peak_2 (~155) and peak_9 (~1111) are weakly constrained (large fractional uncertainties, degenerate eta) and could be partly absorbing residual structure. The higher-shift bands (1206, 1235) are consistent with carbonate but are not individually diagnostic. R2 alone should not be taken as acceptance given the localized apex misfit.
