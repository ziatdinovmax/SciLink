Interpretation

The scientific objective is to resolve distinct sodium environments and flag byproducts in the NLTO product. The data support ONE resolvable 23Na environment: a single narrow, near-symmetric centreband at delta_apparent ~= -4.3 ppm. For 23Na (I=3/2) the isotropic shift is a weak fingerprint (narrow chemical-shift range, offset by field-dependent delta_QIS), so the DISCRIMINATING evidence is the lineshape: a narrow, symmetric line indicates a mobile or high-symmetry (small-EFG, motionally averaged) Na+ site rather than a static, low-symmetry crystallographic site (which would give a broad second-order quadrupolar lineshape). This is consistent with mobile Na+ in an ion conductor. No second-order quadrupolar broadening was resolved and Cq_resolved was not achieved, so no C_Q/eta_Q can be reported as a measurement (only an upper bound). The periodic comb across +/-2000 ppm is a spinning-sideband manifold of this same species with spacing ~87 ppm (nu_r/nu_L), NOT separate byproduct resonances - none of the extra peaks sit off the comb, so no distinct crystalline byproduct (e.g. Na2CO3, NaCl) is cleanly resolved in this single fit. Crucially the fit is only nominally good: the global R^2=0.975 and peak_region_r2=0.980 are inflated by the towering centreband, while the residuals show a coherent comb 10-15x noise (unmodelled higher sidebands) and a large negative trough at the centreband (mismodelled apex/near-wing and unphased negative lobe). The returned negative order_intensities confirm the manifold is compensating rather than fitting the real positive sideband intensity. Because the sidebands are unfit, integrated intensity and centreband fraction (0.94) undercount/mis-partition the true signal, and any quantification of relative Na populations is not defensible from this fit. The MAS rate and nu_L were partly inferred from the comb (metadata sidecar missing), so Hz-based and C_Q-derived values are explicitly provisional.

5. Caveats & Limitations

The fit is not physically acceptable despite passing the R^2 threshold: global R^2 (0.975) and peak_region_r2 (0.980) are dominated by the enormous centreband, while residuals reveal a coherent unmodelled sideband comb (10-15x noise) and a large negative trough / derivative-like misfit at the centreband apex and near-wings. All fitted sideband order_intensities are NEGATIVE while the data sidebands are positive - the manifold is compensating, not fitting, so the reported total_integrated_intensity (5.6e12) and centreband_fraction (0.94) are unreliable and any Na population quantification is undercounted. No second-order quadrupolar lineshape was resolved (Cq_resolved not achieved), so C_Q/eta_Q cannot be reported as measurements - only upper bounds. The MAS rate (~87 ppm) and nu_L were partly derived from the measured comb because the acquisition metadata sidecar was missing, making all Hz-based and C_Q-derived values provisional. The near-centreband negative lobe likely reflects a phasing issue that was not properly re-phased. A single spectrum cannot establish cross-series trends. The narrow-symmetric-line assignment relies on lineshape, not shift, per domain rules; a small unresolved C_Q cannot be excluded.
