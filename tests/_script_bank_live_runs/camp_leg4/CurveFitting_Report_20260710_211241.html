5. Caveats & Limitations

The disclosed model matches the Stage 1 hypothesis, so interpretation is limited by execution details rather than model choice. (1) instrumental_fwhm was set to 0.0, so ALL Scherrer sizes are lower bounds and true domain sizes are larger; report as '>= X nm'. (2) The Williamson-Hall decomposition is invalid (R^2 = 0.16, negative slope implying negative strain) and is NOT reported; the negative/flat slope with poor linearity is the expected symptom of missing instrumental correction plus anisotropy, so no strain value should be claimed. (3) Per-peak sizes vary >3x - anisotropic broadening or possible mixed-phase contributions; the mean is an oversimplification and the 7.5/15-17 nm outliers (broad/overlapped weak peaks) are the least reliable. (4) ~40-sigma derivative residuals persist at the apices of the three sharpest lines (11.9, 17.7, 20 deg); these are the irreducible split-PV profile limit on high-dynamic-range lines, not missing peaks, but they mean the very sharpest peak shapes are imperfectly captured. (5) Several eta values pin at 0 or 1, so Gaussian-Lorentzian mixing is unconstrained for those peaks. (6) The giant ~3.9 deg low-angle feature was excluded (fit lo=5.0) and is not characterized here. (7) Standard parameter uncertainties were not propagated, so numeric error bars are the ~+-15% Scherrer accuracy prior plus the lower-bound caveat, not formal fit errors.
