Per the validation rules, eta pinned at 0/1 typically means the data do not constrain the mixing for those (often weaker or overlapped) peaks; the size numbers derived from their FWHM inherit this uncertainty and should carry the standard +/-15% (or larger) accuracy caveat. Crucially, this is a single frame of an in-situ thermal ramp (31->120 C, 1 K/min). Under the sample-and-mounting priors, a variable-temperature run is exactly the case where the lattice genuinely moves - so peak-center shifts frame-to-frame are the physical signal of interest (thermal expansion, and potentially a phase transition / precursor decomposition en route to metallic Au or an intermediate). The refined centers here are the anchor for tracking that evolution. No phase has yet been assigned; structure matching is the natural next step. The apex misfit at 11.6 deg is accepted as the profile limit and does not affect these conclusions.
