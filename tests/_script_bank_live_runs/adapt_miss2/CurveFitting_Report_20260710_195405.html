No chemistry (EDX/XRF) was supplied, so the assignment is pattern-only but strongly self-consistent across positions AND relative intensities.

5. Caveats & Limitations

Identification is pattern-only: no EDX/XRF chemistry was provided to confirm Mg/Fe/Si, so the element-consistency hard constraint could not be applied. The symmetric-doublet model leaves a large structured residual (+/-40-60 sigma) on the 822/854 crests and over-projects Lorentzian wing intensity into the empty 716-790 cm-1 region; the two strong bands likely carry real asymmetry, so their fitted FWHMs and wing shapes are biased and their areas should be used cautiously. Weak-band eta values pin to rails (0/1) and are unconstrained, and weak-band amplitudes carry 10-25% uncertainty; the 300-330 cluster shows minor under-resolution of fine structure. Exact Fo# is not derived here because no calibrated position-vs-composition curve was applied. Beyond ~1000 cm-1 the OH-stretch region (~3600 cm-1) was not measured, so hydrous phase discrimination is not possible.
