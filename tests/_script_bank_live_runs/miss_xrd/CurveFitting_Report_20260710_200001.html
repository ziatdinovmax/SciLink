5. Caveats & Limitations

Model and data agree (Stage 2 divergence is minimal), but several limitations apply: (1) Williamson-Hall is invalid (R2=0.10, unphysical negative slope) - size+strain decomposition is suppressed per domain rules; only per-peak Scherrer sizes and their RANGE are reported, and the isotropic mean is an oversimplification given >2.5x size spread (anisotropic broadening). (2) Two broad Lorentzian components (peaks 1 and 3, FWHM 1.4-2.0 deg, eta~0.99, Scherrer 3-6 nm) are likely residual background/amorphous scattering absorbed by the peak model, not real crystallite reflections - their sizes should be discarded. (3) The sharpest peaks (22.2, 11 deg) show apex residuals up to ~34 sigma and the 18.6-20.9 deg cluster shows a coherent residual band; these are the irreducible split-PV profile limit and mild overlap, so integrated intensities in those regions carry extra uncertainty. (4) Scherrer sizes are not instrumental-broadening-corrected here, so absolute sizes are upper-limited by the instrument profile; treat as approximate (+/-15% at best). (5) Peaks with eta pinned at 0 or ~1 have unconstrained Gaussian-Lorentzian mixing. (6) The giant ~3.3 deg low-angle feature was (correctly) excluded and is not interpreted.
