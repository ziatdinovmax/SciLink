5. Caveats & Limitations

The symmetric pseudo-Voigt model leaves large coherent residuals (up to ~30-70x noise) on the flanks of the strong 205 and 464 bands, indicating intrinsic band asymmetry not fully captured; the ~120 and ~450 cm-1 'shoulder' components (eta pinned at bounds, large fractional errors) are partly asymmetry-repair terms and should not be over-interpreted as independent physical modes. R^2 ~0.999 is inflated by the many well-fit low-intensity points and does not certify the strong-band lineshapes. No elemental chemistry (EDX/XRF) was provided, so the quartz identification is pattern-only (though multi-band supported) and the accessory-phase assignment (possible carbonate ~1085 or silicate ~810) is tentative and not a complete fingerprint.
