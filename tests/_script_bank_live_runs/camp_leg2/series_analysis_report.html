1. Scientific Analysis

Two in-situ synchrotron XRD patterns during a dehydration ramp were fit with a global split-pseudo-Voigt-per-reflection model on a SNIP background, achieving global R²≈1.0000 and peak-region R²≈0.999 at both temperatures — an excellent, complete fit with no unmodelled-reflection residual and no flags or refits. From 33.4 to 35.9 °C the dominant reflections shift coherently to lower 2θ by ~0.004–0.008° (higher-angle peaks shifting more in absolute 2θ), the direction and angle-dependence expected for a real lattice expansion, which for a variable-temperature run is a legitimately trackable structural change rather than a suspect displacement artifact per the domain sample-and-mounting priors. All dominant integrated areas rise. These trends are directionally coherent but rest on only two temperature points separated by 2.5 °C, so no slope is statistically validated and the intensity rise cannot be cleanly separated from incident-flux/normalization drift. The size/strain analysis is unreliable and should not be over-interpreted: per-peak Scherrer sizes span >3× (~15–44 nm), triggering the anisotropic-broadening flag; the Scherrer mean (~27 nm) disagrees strongly with the W-H size (~46 nm) at 33.4 °C; and W-H size and microstrain drop together, the signature of an ill-conditioned two-point W-H rather than genuine strain relaxation. Additionally, spectrum 0's η values pinned at the [0,1] bounds mean its per-peak profile shape (and thus FWHM-derived sizes) is poorly constrained. The robust, defensible finding is the coherent small shift to lower 2θ consistent with lattice expansion during the dehydration step; the crystallite-size/strain story is not supported at the required confidence.

6. Caveats & Limitations

Only 2 temperature points 2.5 °C apart: all 'trends' are connecting lines, not validated slopes, and direction is the only meaningful indicator. The coherent lower-2θ shift is small (~0.004–0.008°), at the level where sample displacement/zero-shift cannot be fully excluded, though the same-sign angle-dependent pattern favors a real cell change in this in-situ run. Peak-area increases cannot be separated from incident-flux/normalization drift without a monitored intensity reference. Crystallite size/strain: per-peak Scherrer sizes vary by >3× (anisotropic broadening flag), Scherrer mean and W-H size disagree (~27 vs ~46 nm), and W-H size and microstrain drop together — indicating an unstable/ill-conditioned decomposition; per domain rules the W-H strain-size decomposition should not be reported as physical, only the per-peak size range (~15–44 nm) noted. Model-vs-evidence divergence: spectrum 0 returns many η values pinned at the [0,1] bounds (unconstrained Gaussian/Lorentzian mixing), so its shape-derived FWHM/size quantities carry extra uncertainty even though the global fit is excellent. Phase identification has not been performed; recommend running structure_matching/xrd on the same patterns (passing the refined FWHMs) before making any crystallographic claim.
