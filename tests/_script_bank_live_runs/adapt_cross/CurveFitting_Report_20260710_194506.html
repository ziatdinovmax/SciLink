5. Caveats & Limitations

No chemistry (EDX/XRF) was provided, so the identification is pattern-only; Ti/O could not be confirmed independently. The rutile assignment rests on band positions and relative intensities matching the rutile fingerprint (447/612 doublet, broad 232, no strong 144); a confirming measurement would strengthen it. The Eg-band decomposition into a 442 core plus 405/448 shoulders is a mathematical asymmetry-absorbing superposition, NOT three physical phonons: those subcomponents have eta pinned to 0/1 boundaries and large amplitude uncertainties (+/-337 to +/-1517), so their individual areas are unreliable and only the composite Eg quantity should be quoted. Systematic ~3 sigma residuals persist at the strong bands (143, 232, 445, 611), indicating that symmetric pseudo-Voigt profiles under-represent the true anharmonic/asymmetric lineshapes; an asymmetric (e.g. skewed/Fano) profile might have fit the same data with fewer components and revealed the intrinsic band asymmetry. R2=0.9993 alone does not validate the component count given these structured residuals.
