Interpretation

The spectrum is unambiguously the Fe L2,3 ionization edge measured by EELS (100 kV, alpha 7.5 mrad, beta 24 mrad), consistent with the Fe+O metadata. The L3 white line is centered at 710.58 +/- 0.01 eV and the L2 near 723.85 +/- 0.12 eV, a ~13.3 eV spin-orbit splitting typical of Fe. The L3 position (~710.5-710.6 eV) is characteristic of oxidized Fe (Fe3+-rich, as in Fe2O3/oxyhydroxides), well above the ~708 eV of metallic Fe. A resolved shoulder/pre-peak at 708.7 eV (present in the raw data at ~390 counts) is consistent with Fe2+ or crystal-field/multiplet fine structure in a mixed-valence oxide; it is a real feature, not a fit artifact. However, the quantitative fit is compromised in three ways that the residuals expose plainly. First, the L3 main Gaussian (FWHM 2.01 eV) peaks at ~880 while the data reaches ~1010, producing the diagnostic derivative-like residual (+130/-110) centered on the maximum. This is a lineshape failure (symmetric Gaussian on an intrinsically Lorentzian/asymmetric white line), so the fitted FWHM and amplitude are biased and should not be interpreted as physical linewidths. Second, the extended L3 intensity from ~712-718 eV is not captured, giving a broad positive residual and biasing the L3 area. Third, step_L2 is numerically degenerate (height 8.9e-9, edge_err ~2e9), meaning the L2 continuum edge is effectively absent; the fit floor sits near 50 counts at 727-730 eV where the data returns to ~0-10, a clear flat-offset residual. Because both the L3 area is peak-biased and the L2 continuum is missing, the reported L3/L2 area ratio of 8.63 is not reliable as an oxidation-state metric (physically Fe oxides give ~4-6 in white-line-only integration schemes; 8.6 is inflated and consistent with the modeling defects rather than a real chemical value). The qualitative conclusion (oxidized Fe, mixed valence signature) is robust; the quantitative valence-from-ratio conclusion is not.

5. Caveats & Limitations

This fit meets R2=0.95 only because a large flat pre-edge dominates the metric; R2 does not arbitrate the local lineshape failures. Three concrete divergences from an adequate model: (1) the L3 main peak is underfit (model ~880 vs data ~1010) due to using a symmetric Gaussian for an intrinsically asymmetric/Lorentzian white line, producing the +130/-110 derivative residual and rendering fitted L3 FWHM/amplitude unreliable; (2) extended L3 intensity from 712-718 eV is unmodeled, biasing L3 area; (3) step_L2 is degenerate (height 8.9e-9, edge_err ~2e9), so the L2 continuum is effectively missing and the post-L2 baseline sits at ~50 vs data ~0-10. As a direct consequence the reported L3/L2 ratio of 8.63 is an artifact-inflated value and must not be used for quantitative valence determination. The absolute L3 and L2 center energies and the presence of the 708.7 eV shoulder are the most defensible results; the areas and ratio are not.
