Because the dI/dV signal is proportional to the local density of electronic states (LDOS), features at negative bias reflect filled (occupied) states and features at positive bias reflect empty (unoccupied) states. It is important to note that these components are exploratory tools: they are orthogonal eigenvectors of the data variance that capture where and how the spectra vary across the sample, and they do NOT represent pure, physical electronic phases or absolute LDOS spectra.
